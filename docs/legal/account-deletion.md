You can delete your Petsitters account, made by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], at any time.

## In the app

1. Open Petsitters and sign in.
2. Tap **My profile** (at the bottom of your home screen).
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

## Without the app

Email [privacy@EXAMPLE.COM] from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and delete it within [30] days.

## What is deleted

**Pet owners.** These are deleted straight away:

- your account and sign-in;
- your home and its access details;
- your emergency contacts;
- your pets and their health details;
- your bookings, visits and care logs;
- your photos, videos and messages.

**Sitters.** Your account, sign-in and profile are deleted. The visits you did stay in the business's records without your name. The business needs them for payroll and for its records about the pets in its care.

**Business admins.** If you are the last admin of a business that still has clients or sitters, hand over or remove them first, or email us to close the business.

- Backups are overwritten within [7] days.
- Error reports, which have no personal details, are kept for up to [90] days.
- We may keep records we are legally required to keep, for as long as the law requires.
