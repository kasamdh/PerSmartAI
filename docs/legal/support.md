## Common questions

**How do I join my pet-sitting business?** Ask them for an invite code. In the app, tap **I have an invite code** and enter it.

**Why does the app ask for my location?** Only sitters are asked, and only when they check in or out of a visit, so the owner knows the visit happened at their home. The app never tracks location in the background. You can say no and still check in.

**Why does the app ask for notifications?** So owners hear when their sitter arrives and finishes, and sitters get visit reminders. You can turn individual alerts off with the **Notifications** button on your home screen.

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: **My profile > Delete my account**. See [delete your account](/account-deletion).

**Where is the privacy policy?** See the [privacy policy](/privacy) and [terms of service](/terms).
