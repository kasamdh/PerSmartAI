Pet Sitter is an app and website that pet-sitting businesses use to run their visits, and that their clients and sitters use to book, carry out and follow those visits. It is provided by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS] ("we", "us").

## Who is responsible for your data

- **If you are a client or a sitter of a pet-sitting business**, that business decides what it uses Pet Sitter for and is responsible for your data (in GDPR terms, the "controller"). We store and process it for them (the "processor"). For most questions, contact the business first; we will help them answer.
- **If you run a business on Pet Sitter**, we are responsible for your account data and for the data we need to run and secure the service.

## What we collect

**Account details**

- Your name, email address, phone number (if you add it) and role (pet owner, sitter or business admin).
- Your password, which is stored only as a secure hash.

**Business details**

- The business name, logo, brand colors, time zone and scheduling settings.

**Home details**

Pet owners add these so that sitters can do the visit:

- Home address, and the home's map location if one is added.
- Entry instructions, alarm notes and Wi-Fi details.
- Emergency contacts: names, phone numbers and relationship. Please only add people who agree to be contacted.

**Pet details, including health information**

- Name, species, breed, sex, birth date, weight, microchip number, photo and temperament notes.
- Vet name, phone and address.
- Allergies and reactions, medications and doses, and feeding plans.

**Bookings and visits**

- Requested and confirmed dates and times, and who the sitter is.
- Visit status, check-in and check-out times, and the sitter's short live status and visit summary.

**Check-in location**

- When a sitter checks in or out of a visit, the app records the phone's location **at that moment only**. This confirms the sitter is at the pet's home.
- The app never tracks location in the background or between visits.
- Sitters can refuse location access and still check in.

**Care log and incidents**

- Feeding, water, walks, potty breaks, medications given or skipped, notes, and incident reports.

**Photos and videos**

- Photos and videos taken or chosen during visits, and pet profile photos. They may show the inside of a home or people.
- Who is tagged in them, and "hearts" (reactions) from owners.

**Messages**

- Messages and photos sent in a household's chat, and when each person last read them.

**Device and notification data**

- A push notification token for your device and your notification settings.

**Error reports**

- When the app or website crashes or hits an error, we receive a technical report: the error, app version, device model and operating system, and your user ID.
- We remove names, addresses, messages, notes, contact details, access codes and similar content before reports leave your device.

**Usage statistics**

- We count a few anonymous events (a booking was created, a visit was completed, a photo was sent) with no personal details attached and no way to identify you.
- We do not use advertising identifiers.

**Security logs**

- Our hosting provider receives your IP address with each request.
- We keep short-lived records to limit repeated sign-in and invite-code attempts (deleted within a day).

We do not collect payment details. Payments, if any, are between you and the pet-sitting business, outside Pet Sitter.

## How we use it

- To provide the service: scheduling, visits, care logs, report cards, photos, messages and notifications.
- To confirm that visits happened (check-in location and times).
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
- To find and fix errors, and to understand which features are used (anonymous statistics only).
- To contact you about your account or important changes.

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Who can see it

**Inside a business**

- **Pet owners** see their own home, pets, bookings, visits, care logs, photos and their household's messages.
- **Sitters** see the pets and homes they are assigned to. They see entry instructions, alarm and Wi-Fi details only from 24 hours before a visit until 24 hours after it. They can message a household only around their visits (30 days before or after).
- **Business admins** see all of their business's clients, pets, visits, photos and messages, and can export sitters' hours for payroll.
- **Other businesses never see your data.**

**Service providers**

We use these providers to run Pet Sitter. They process data only on our instructions:

- [Supabase Inc.] – database, file storage, sign-in and hosting ([REGION]).
- [Expo / 650 Industries] – sends push notifications. Notification text can include a pet's name and visit status.
- [Apple and Google] – deliver push notifications to devices.
- [Cloudflare, Inc.] – the security check on sign-in and sign-up (Turnstile). It sees your IP address and browser or device signals for a moment, to tell people from bots.
- [Functional Software Inc. (Sentry)] – error reports, with personal details removed.
- [PostHog Inc.] – anonymous usage statistics.
- [EMAIL PROVIDER] – sign-in and account emails.
- [WEB HOSTING PROVIDER] – hosts the business website.

**When the law requires it**

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

## How long we keep it

- **Account, home, pet and booking data** is kept while the account is active.
- **Photos and videos** from visits are kept for as long as the pet-sitting business chooses. Businesses can set automatic deletion after 3, 6, 12 or 24 months, or keep them.
- **Messages** are kept until the sender or an admin deletes them, or the household is deleted.
- **Error reports** are kept for [90] days, and **usage statistics** for [12] months.
- **Rate-limit records** are kept for up to 1 day.
- **Backups** are kept for up to [7] days and then overwritten.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** **My profile**, then **Delete my account**.
  - **Pet owners:** deleting your account deletes your home, pets, bookings, visits, care logs, photos and messages.
  - **Sitters:** deleting your account removes your profile and sign-in. The visits you did stay in the business's records without your name, because the business needs them, for example for payroll and its records about the pets in its care.
  - **The last admin of a business:** you must first remove or hand over its members.
- **By email:** write to [privacy@EXAMPLE.COM] from the address on your account. We will delete within [30] days.
- See also our [account deletion page](/account-deletion).

Deleted data disappears from backups as they are overwritten (within [7] days).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- see the data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- restrict or object to how it is used.

To use these rights, contact the pet-sitting business, or us at [privacy@EXAMPLE.COM]. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Security

- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider.
- Each business's data is separated by database rules, and we test those rules automatically.
- Photos are shared only through links that expire after an hour.
- No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password and tell us about any concern at [security@EXAMPLE.COM].
