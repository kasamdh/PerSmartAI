**Notes for the lawyer (delete before publishing)**

- Businesses using Pet Sitter should sign a business agreement that covers:
  - fees;
  - a data processing agreement (we process client and sitter data for them);
  - their duty to have a lawful basis and to give notice to their clients and sitters;
  - their responsibility for sitters' access to homes.
- Pet health information is not human health data, but owners may add sensitive notes. Consider whether any state law (for example Washington's My Health My Data Act) is relevant.
- Location is collected only at check-in and check-out. Confirm whether it counts as "precise geolocation" under applicable state laws, and whether it needs opt-in consent.
